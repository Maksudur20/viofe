The Maison

Slow hands.
Lasting things.

Our belief

VIOFE was born from a simple wish: that the most beautiful things a woman wears should also be the most honest — made slowly, by hand, by people who are proud to sign their work.

I

Made by hand

Every saree is woven on a loom and every stone is set by a person, never a machine.

II

Honest materials

Pure silk, tested zari and nickel-free metals — certified, and always disclosed.

III

Made to be kept

We design for decades, not seasons, and offer lifetime care on every piece.

Our journey

From one loom to a maison

  1. 2019

    A single loom

    VIOFE begins as one commissioned bridal saree for a friend.

  2. 2021

    The jewellery atelier

    We partner with a third-generation kundan family workshop.

  3. 2024

    140 artisans

    Our network grows across Varanasi, Kanchipuram and Dhaka.

  4. 2026

    The Heirloom Edit

    Our first complete collection of sarees and jewellery.
